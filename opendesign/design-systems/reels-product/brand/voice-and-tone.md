# Voice and tone

- **Who:** Solo local operator cutting VODs into clips and publishing.
- **Voice:** Direct, operational, short. Portuguese UI.
- **Casing:** Sentence case for buttons and labels (`Abrir galeria`, not Title Case).
- **Brand about itself:** Product name `Reels.` — amber period is part of the mark.
- **Emoji:** Do not use as icons or feature decoration.
- **Numbers:** Duration as `m:ss` / `h:mm:ss`; file size compact (`12.4 MB`).
- **Errors:** Plain cause + next action (`Falha ao transformar em reel`).
